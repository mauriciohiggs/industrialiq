# IndustrialIQ roadmap

## Current

- Industrial Automation > Ignition Core: nine topic-based learning modules, 72 original questions, flashcards, practice, exam exercises, analytics, and local progress.
- TypeScript graph interfaces and an Ignition Core course catalog derived from the existing question bank.
- Standalone illustrative readiness calculation and deterministic mock mentor; neither is connected to learner progress or displayed as an assessment.

## In Development

- Review and curate skills, competencies, and question-to-skill mappings; categories are not automatically competencies.
- Define evidence requirements and evaluate an explainable readiness approach before connecting it to the app.

## Planned

- Cybersecurity, Engineering, and Manufacturing courses.
- An opt-in, source-grounded AI mentor with privacy, safety, and evaluation controls.
- Certification and career-path mappings, persistent graph versioning, and optional accounts and synchronization.
- Any calibrated certification prediction requires representative outcome data and validation; practice scores are not a pass probability.
