IndustrialIQ
Privacy

Your progress stays on your device.

Effective November 23, 2026

IndustrialIQ is a static learning application. It does not require an account and does not send study progress to an IndustrialIQ server.

Data stored in your browser

Quiz answers, flashcard schedules, and activity dates are stored in your browser's local storage. They remain associated with the site origin and browser profile where you use the app. Exported progress files are created locally by your browser.

Tracking and third parties

The app contains no advertising or analytics trackers. Fonts and application assets are bundled with the release. Opening an official reference navigates to that third-party website, whose policies apply there.

Hosting

The hosting provider may process standard request data, including IP addresses and browser metadata, to deliver and secure the site. It does not receive the progress stored in your browser.

Your choices

You can export, import, or clear progress from the Community & sharing section. Clearing site data in your browser also removes local progress.
